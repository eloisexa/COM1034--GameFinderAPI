As a time-limited gamer, I want to manage and prioritise my game wishlist so that I can easily decide what to play next based on my interests and available time.

## Personas

<!-- You should have 2 personas. Each persona should include a name, a photo, a short bio, and a list of goals and frustrations. -->

Persona 1: 

Alex Turner, age 29, is a UX designer for a large business based in Guildford UK. He doesn’t spend much time gaming per week, roughly 5-8 hours in the evenings and weekends. Originally being a PC gamer, he has decided to focus more on quick, casual gaming on his PS5 and Nintendo Switch. Outside of gaming, Alex lives a fairly balanced and creative life. Weekends are often split between catching up with friends, going for long walks and diving into a good story-driven game when time allows. Alex also enjoys music and podcasts, often pairing them with late-night gaming sessions, treating those moments as a way to decompress and recharge before the week ahead. 

Alex loves games but struggles with too many choices and too little time. Between work, social life, and other hobbies, gaming competes for limited free time. Alex often discovers new titles through reviews, social media, and friends but ends up overwhelmed by a growing mental list of “games to play someday.” He wishes to keep track of games of his interest without forgetting about them and prioritise what to play next based on factors such as his mood and the amount of time he has to play. Overall, avoiding wasting time deciding what he should play next. 

Persona 2: 

Priya Shah, age 24, is a marketing assistant for a small, local company based in London UK. Her time spent gaming is often in short bursts on her mobile phone or PS5, and she often spends her gaming time socially with friends online. She lives with two friends in a lively part of London, and her evenings are often filled with spontaneous plans—dinners, drinks, or scrolling through social media to keep up with trends. She loves creating content, whether it’s short videos, aesthetic photos, or sharing recommendations with her followers. Fitness classes and weekend brunches are regular fixtures in her routine, but she always keeps her phone close—ready to jump into a game session if friends are online. 

Priya’s gaming is heavily influenced by what’s trending and what her friends are playing. She enjoys being part of conversations - whether that’s discussing the latest release, sharing clips, or joining multiplayer sessions. Her wishlist isn’t personal, it’s socially driven. She often saves games based on friends’ recommendations and trending games online. Her goals are to keep track of what games her friends are talking about, quickly decide what games to play when friends invite her, prioritise games that are currently in trend and stay in the loop without feeling overwhelmed.
